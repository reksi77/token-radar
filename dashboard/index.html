How the implied value is derived

How to farm it

    This protocol records no token in the snapshot. That is the record, not proof: it may still have a token the record does not carry. Farming costs real money in fees and carries the protocol's own risks — never farm with money you cannot afford to lose.

    How the value is modelled

    Every implied value on this board is computed from the snapshot's own figures by impliedValue in lib/model.ts:

    implied value = TVL × category multiple × growth factor × age factor

    • Category multiple — the median market-cap-to-TVL ratio of the tokenized protocols in the same category, shrunk toward the global median when a category has few tokenized peers, clamped to 0.02–2.00. Categories whose tokens trade at a high multiple of TVL are categories whose next token is worth more. Computed by categoryMultiples, never from a list of names.
    • Growth factor — 1 plus twice the protocol's 7-day TVL change, clamped to 0.70–1.50. Fast-growing TVL is worth more than flat TVL; an unreported change scores 1.00.
    • Age factor — 1 plus 0.05 per year listed with no token, capped at 1.20. A protocol that ran for years with no token has a longer record and more pent-up launch value; the factor is deliberately small so TVL and the category multiple do the ranking, not age alone.

    Protocols whose token already trades are not ranked as candidates: they are the peers the multiples are computed from, and the board holds pre-token protocols only. The watchlist (40 protocols at or above the $50M floor) is the floor, never the ceiling — the board also ranks the early pre-token protocols below it.

    Source: DefiLlama protocol data, snapshot of 14 November 2026. Implied values are this board's own claim, not a price, a prediction of whether or when a token is issued, or investment advice.
