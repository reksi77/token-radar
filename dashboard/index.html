How the implied value is derived

Farm economics

How to farm it

    This protocol records no token in the snapshot. That is the record, not proof: it may still have a token the record does not carry. Farming costs real money in fees and carries the protocol's own risks — never farm with money you cannot afford to lose.

    How the value is modelled

    Every implied value on this board is fitted from the snapshot's own figures by fitModel in lib/model.ts (ridge, alpha = 1.0, on the 95 traded tokens with a complete record):

    log(mcap / TVL) = −1.375 − 0.530 × (log TVL − 17) − 0.280 × (chg / 10)

    • Size curve — log(mcap/TVL) falls with log TVL (correlation −0.471). A $100M-TVL protocol's token trades at ~0.119× TVL; a $1B one at ~0.035×. The old flat median multiple ignored this and inflated giants.
    • 7-day change — clipped to ±50%, a +10-point change lowers the multiple slightly (coefficient −0.280 per +10pp). Growth does not raise the multiple in the data; the fitted sign is negative, so the model follows it.
    • Token odds — a Weibull hazard on age (lam 2.90 yr, k 0.70): the longer a protocol runs with no token, the less likely it launches one in the next 2 years. Odds at age 1yr: 0.49; at 3yr: 0.38.
    • Tested, not kept — category, age, chain count and size² were all tried and dropped because they worsened the walk-forward test; see the evidence tables above. A permuted-change control does not help, as it should not.

    The full method, with every held-out number, is in dashboard/00-method.md. Protocols whose token already trades are not ranked as candidates: they are the peers the model is fitted from, and the board holds pre-token protocols only. The watchlist (40 protocols at or above the $50M floor) is the floor, never the ceiling — the board also ranks the early pre-token protocols below it.
